Delete your account

Last updated: 10 October 2026

You can delete your Taiyari account and data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Taiyari and log in.
  2. Tap your name at the top to open My progress.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

Write to from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject Delete my Taiyari account. We aim to delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, profile (name, date of birth, gender)
  • Your quiz history and answers
  • Your saved notes
  • Your AI usage records

Reports you sent about questions may be kept without any link to your account. Data stored in your own browser or app is removed when you log out or clear the app's storage.

See also the Privacy Policy.
