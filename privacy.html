Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Taiyari is a free exam-practice app with quizzes, your own notes, and AI-made practice questions. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Questions: .

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: email address, password (kept only as a secure hash by our login service; we cannot read it), name, date of birth and gender.
  • Your activity: the quizzes you take, your answers, scores and dates.
  • Content you add: notes you save (title and text) and the exam or topic you type for AI practice.
  • Usage limits: the time of each AI request, so we can apply a daily limit.
  • Reports: questions you report as wrong or inappropriate.
  • On your device: your language choice, your login session and a copy of your history, kept in your browser or app storage.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID.

2. How we use it

To create your account, send login codes, save your progress, show your history, make AI questions when you ask for them, apply fair-use limits, review reports, and keep the app safe. We do not sell your data.

3. Services that help us run Taiyari

  • Supabase – login and database hosting.
  • Resend – sending your email login codes.
  • Google Gemini API – when you tap Practice with AI or AI practice, the text of your note or the topic you typed is sent to Google to write questions. On Google's free tier, Google may use content to improve its products and people may review it. Please do not put private or sensitive information in notes you use with AI.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, Cloudflare cdnjs – deliver the app files, fonts and the PDF reader library. They may see your IP address when files load.

PDF files are read inside your browser. The PDF itself is not uploaded; only the text you choose to save is stored.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself in the app (My progress → Delete my account) or by following the steps on the delete account page. Deleting removes your profile, quiz history, notes and usage records from our live database. Reports you sent may be kept without any link to your account.

5. Children

Taiyari is for people aged 13 and over. If you are younger, please do not create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, and database rules let each person read only their own records. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to . Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
