Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Taiyari is a free exam-practice app for students in India. This page explains what the app collects, why, and your choices. Contact: .

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a password (kept only in scrambled form by our login provider), your name, date of birth and gender.
  • Your study data: quiz results, the questions and answers from your quizzes, and notes you save.
  • AI requests: when you use "Practice with AI" or "AI practice", the note text or the exam and topic you type is sent to Google's Gemini service to create questions.
  • Reports and usage counts: if you report a question we store it. We also count your AI quizzes per day to keep the service free for everyone.
  • On your device: the app keeps a copy of your data and language choice in your phone's browser storage so it loads quickly.

Why we use it

To log you in, save your progress across devices, show your quiz history, make questions from your notes, keep the service secure and fix problems. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and login system.
  • Resend sends the verification code emails.
  • Google (Gemini API) creates AI questions, only when you choose an AI feature. Do not put private or sensitive information in notes you send to AI. Google's terms apply to that processing.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all its data inside the app (My progress, then Delete my account) or follow the steps on the delete account page. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

Taiyari is meant for students aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please do not create an account. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

AI-made questions

Questions made by AI can contain mistakes. Always check answers with your books or official papers. You can report a question inside the app.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. See also our Terms of Use.
